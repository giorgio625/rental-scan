I read "Chicago River / Clybourn corridor" as the fuzzy corridor the criteria's own wording implies and counted this in-bounds (Lincoln Park, secondary, −5 if scored). Read as a strict line at Clybourn Ave, this address falls in the gap between both boxes and would fail location too, on top of availability. Worth your own eyes on this one regardless — if either unit's window moves, this is the pick of today's litter.
